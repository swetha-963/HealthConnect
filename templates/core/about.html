About HealthConnect

One platform for clinical appointments and pharmacy delivery.

Our mission

We make routine healthcare simple: find the right doctor, reserve a slot that fits your day, and restock your medicine cabinet without a trip to the pharmacy.

How we work

Every doctor profile, schedule and medicine listing is curated by our admin team. Appointments are reviewed and confirmed before your visit, and orders are tracked from packing to delivery.

Patient safety

Prescription-only medicines are clearly flagged, stock levels are updated on every order, and your personal data stays inside your account.
